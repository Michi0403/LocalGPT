LocalGPT 2.1.16 / LocalGpt(38) targeted static review
=====================================================

Purpose
-------
This patch release addresses the Windows/.NET 10 semantic compiler finding
reported after the 2.1.15 iterator and generated-artifact corrections.

Reported failure addressed
--------------------------
ProjectMaintenanceService.EvaluateAccessPolicyRule used:

    entries.Where(regex.IsMatch)

Regex.IsMatch exposes overloads that can match both the ordinary and indexed
Enumerable.Where predicate shapes during method-group conversion. The .NET 10
compiler therefore reported CS0121 at ProjectMaintenanceService.cs:1018.

Resolution
----------
The matcher now uses an explicit one-argument lambda:

    entries.Where(entry => regex.IsMatch(entry))

This deterministically selects Where(Func<string, bool>). The existing regex,
100-entry bound, permission findings, severities and access-policy behavior are
unchanged.
