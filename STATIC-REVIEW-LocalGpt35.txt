- LocalGPT.dll can therefore be emitted; LocalGPTWebviewWrapper no longer fails with the cascading WMC1006/CS0006 missing-reference errors.
- The owner reported a full Debug rebuild after this correction: 4 succeeded, 0 failed.

Database SaveChanges/startup isolation
- DatabaseLoggerProvider now waits on IDatabaseLoggerReadiness before opening a DbContext or writing ApplicationLogs.
- DatabaseInitializationService opens that one-way gate only after health checks, compatibility preparation, EF migration, and all deterministic seed stages have completed.
- Startup diagnostics remain bounded in the existing channel and flush after readiness; other configured providers continue to receive them immediately.
- If initialization never succeeds, provider disposal cancels the dormant worker rather than attempting a final database flush against an unavailable or migrating schema.
- This targets the concrete startup race where database-backed logging could issue SaveChangesAsync while migration or seed SaveChangesAsync still owned SQLite.
