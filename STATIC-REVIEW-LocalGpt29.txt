LocalGpt(29) / LocalGPT 2.0.4 static review
============================================

Scope
-----
This revision addresses the build-policy failures and runtime diagnostics failure reported after LocalGpt(28):

1. TestLab direct Split/string.Join ownership findings.
2. Architecture-audit stdout incorrectly becoming the PowerShell return value.
3. Async continuation policy findings in Razor components and background services.
4. Application-static findings in structured-text services.
5. Iterator exception-policy findings in RemoteKnowledgeImportService.
6. Newly introduced data definitions nested inside diagnostics/services.
7. Missing logged exception boundaries in the newly added game/import services and controllers.
8. ServiceMethodLoggingDispatchProxy disposing or invalidating DI-owned service targets.
9. Core-project seed DbUpdateConcurrencyException handling.
10. Excessive zero-millisecond service-operation log traffic.

Implemented review decisions
----------------------------
- The build and architecture checks remain enabled. Their implementation/baselines were corrected rather than removed.
- New mutable/snapshot/session/candidate data types live in BusinessObjects. Service classes own orchestration and behavior.
- ServiceMethodLoggingDispatchProxy is not disposable and never owns or disposes the service target.
- Disposable implementation types are not proxied; DI remains the only lifetime owner.
- Successful hot-path calls remain available at Trace and are summarized at Information in bounded batches. Failures and cancellations are logged immediately with their exception.
- Remote-import and game services/controllers have explicit logged exception boundaries. Remote URLs/content, prompts and frames are omitted from logs.
- Seed concurrency reconciliation reloads the durable row and keeps user/database values authoritative before saving unrelated additive seed changes.
